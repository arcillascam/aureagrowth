Full Cold Call Script

UK premium clinics · Aesthetics, dental, and wellness · Gatekeeper to decision-maker flow included. Read naturally — do not recite.

1

Gatekeeper — Getting Through

Receptionist or front desk answers

Opening ▼
What to say
"Hi there — my name is [Name], calling from Aurea Growth. Could I speak with the practice manager or the clinic owner, please?"

— If they ask what it's regarding —

"We work with aesthetics and dental clinics on their enquiry and booking systems — specifically helping clinics reduce missed enquiries and no-shows. I just wanted a brief word with whoever handles that side of the business."
💡 Stay calm and matter-of-fact. Do not over-explain to the gatekeeper. The goal is to get transferred, not to pitch. If they push back, simply ask: "Is there a better time to reach them directly?"
2

Decision-Maker Opens

Owner, practice manager, or clinic director answers

Opener ▼
Opening — 20 seconds
"Hi [Name] — I'll keep this short. My name is [Caller Name] from Aurea Growth. We work with premium clinics across the UK — mainly aesthetics, dental, and wellness — helping them close the gap between first enquiry and attended consultation.

Most clinics we speak to aren't losing patients because of bad treatments — they're losing them because of slow follow-up, missed calls, or enquiries going cold before anyone responds. That's the problem we fix.

I'm not going to pitch you on the call — I just want to ask one quick question, if that's alright?"
💡 Always ask permission before the question. It resets the dynamic. Most people will say yes, and it signals you're respectful of their time — which matches the Aurea brand.
3

The One Diagnostic Question

Opens the conversation without sounding like a pitch

Diagnostic ▼
The question
"When a new enquiry comes in — whether that's a call, a form, a WhatsApp, or a DM — how quickly does someone from your team actually follow up?"
💡 Then stop. Let them answer fully. Do not interrupt. Their answer tells you everything: whether the pain is real, how self-aware they are, and which service fits. Listen for hesitation, vagueness, or frustration — those are your signals.
If they say "pretty quickly" or "same day"
"That's good to hear. And when you do follow up — is it usually handled by one person, or does it come through a few different channels that can sometimes get missed?"
If they pause, say "it depends", or admit it's inconsistent
"That's honestly the most common thing we hear. Clinics aren't losing enquiries because they're bad at their work — it's usually because the follow-up system isn't built to handle the volume across all the different channels. That's exactly the gap we close."
4

Brief Positioning — What Aurea Actually Does

Only after they've spoken — 60 seconds max

Positioning ▼
Short version — use this first
"What we do is build the enquiry and booking system that sits between a clinic's first contact and the actual attended consultation. That covers things like bringing all the enquiry channels into one inbox, automating follow-up so no enquiry goes cold, qualifying patients before they reach the diary, and reducing no-shows with confirmation and reminder sequences.

We're not an ads agency — we don't start with traffic. We start by finding where enquiries are dropping, and building only what's needed to fix it."
Aesthetics-specific angle (use if applicable)
"For aesthetics clinics in particular, the challenge is usually that enquiries come in from Instagram, WhatsApp, the website, and sometimes phone calls all at once — and there's no single system handling it. So serious enquiries get lost between channels, or they go cold because the response takes hours."
Dental-specific angle (use if applicable)
"For dental clinics, the issue we see most often is missed calls during treatment hours, and then no real follow-up system to recapture those patients. A missed call without a callback sequence is just a lost booking."
Wellness-specific angle (use if applicable)
"For wellness clinics, it tends to be enquiries that come in genuinely interested but then go quiet — and without a proper follow-up or reactivation system in place, those patients either book elsewhere or don't book at all."
5

The Ask — Demo Request

Close for the 30-minute system demo, not a sales call

Close ▼
Primary ask
"What I'd love to do is book a quick 30-minute system demo with you. Before that call, we'd review your current enquiry flow and come to the conversation prepared — so it's not a generic pitch, it's a look at what's actually happening in your clinic and whether we can genuinely help.

There's no obligation — if we're not the right fit, we'll tell you that on the call. Would that be worth 30 minutes of your time?"
If they want more info first
"Completely understand. I can send over a short overview of what we do and a link to our website — it's all there without the fluff. And if it makes sense after you've had a look, we can find a time. What's the best email to send that to?"
💡 Always leave with something — a booked demo, an email address, or a specific callback time. Never end a call without a next step confirmed.
6

Objection Handling Guide

Common responses from UK clinic owners and practice managers — with calm, on-brand replies. Never argue. Always redirect to the real problem.

❌"We already have a system for that."
"That's good to hear — most clinics do have something in place. What we usually find is it's not the tools that are the issue, it's whether they're all connected and whether follow-up is actually happening consistently across every channel. Would you say every enquiry that comes in gets a response within a couple of hours, regardless of how it arrives? If yes, you probably don't need us. If there's any doubt, that's worth a 30-minute look."
❌"We're not looking for more leads right now."
"We're not a leads agency — that's actually not where we start. What we focus on is what happens to the enquiries you're already getting. Most clinics are generating enough interest — the problem is that a percentage of those enquiries go cold before anyone responds, or they book but don't show. That's lost revenue without spending a penny more on marketing."
❌"We've tried agencies before and it didn't work."
"I hear that a lot, and honestly it's usually for the same reason — agencies send traffic into a booking journey that wasn't ready for it. We start before the ads. We look at what happens between first contact and the attended consultation. If that flow isn't right, more leads just create more chaos. We're not going to pitch a growth campaign if the booking system can't support it."
❌"We're too busy right now."
"Completely understand — that's actually a sign things are moving, which is good. The clinics that tend to benefit most from what we do are the busy ones — because that's when missed enquiries and no-shows start costing the most. I'm not asking for much time now — just 30 minutes when it suits you, and we'll come prepared so it's not wasted."
❌"What does it cost?"
"Completely fair question, and I'll be straight with you — it depends on what the clinic actually needs. We don't use fixed packages because not every clinic has the same bottleneck. That's the whole point of the 30-minute demo — we look at your enquiry flow first, then we tell you honestly what we'd recommend and what that looks like commercially. No obligation on the call."
❌"Can you just send me something over?"
"Of course — I'll send you a short overview and our website. I'll keep it concise, no long brochures. And if it looks relevant after you've had a read, we can find a time for a proper conversation. What's the best email?"

→ Then follow up with the email sequence on the Follow-up tab, and book a callback for 2–3 days later.
❌"We handle everything in-house."
"That's great — and we're not here to replace your team. What we build sits alongside your team — it's the system that makes sure no enquiry slips through when the team is busy, in treatments, or off shift. Most of the leakage happens outside of core hours or across channels that aren't being monitored consistently."
❌"I need to speak to my business partner / manager first."
"Absolutely — that makes sense for a decision like this. Would it be worth getting both of you on the 30-minute call? That way we're all in the same room, we can look at the actual flow together, and your partner can ask anything directly. I can find a time that works for both of you if that's easier."

Lead Qualification Guide

Not every clinic is a fit. Use this to decide quickly whether to pursue, which service to position, or to move on.

✅ Strong Fit — Pursue

  • →Premium aesthetics, dental, or wellness clinic
  • →Receives 10+ enquiries per week across multiple channels
  • →Has a team managing bookings (not just the owner)
  • →Mentions missed calls, slow follow-up, or no-shows as pain
  • →Running or considering paid advertising
  • →Owner or practice manager is on the call
  • →Aware that their system has gaps

❌ Poor Fit — Deprioritise

  • →Solo practitioner with very low enquiry volume
  • →Price-led clinic competing mainly on discount
  • →No interest in systems — wants a simple ads campaign only
  • →Already fully booked with no capacity to grow
  • →No budget conversation possible — pure cost focus
  • →Gatekeeper with no access to decision-maker

Which Service to Lead With

Use what they say on the call to place them correctly — before any proposal is discussed.

1

Lead with Booking Control

They mention any of these signals on the call

▼
  • →Enquiries arrive across multiple channels with no central system
  • →Missed calls are a regular issue
  • →Follow-up relies on the team remembering or checking manually
  • →No-shows happen frequently with no reduction system in place
  • →Bookings come through an open calendar link with no qualification
  • →They're not running ads yet or ads have been inconsistent
2

Lead with Growth & Retention

They mention any of these signals on the call

▼
  • →Booking journey is already organised and follow-up is consistent
  • →Running paid ads but conversion or tracking is unclear
  • →Have a patient database but no reactivation system
  • →Want to grow specific treatments or add demand deliberately
  • →Reviews are sparse or Google profile is underused
  • →Ready to invest in a full growth system, not just a quick fix
